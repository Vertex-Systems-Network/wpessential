## Research rules

1. A benchmark claim must point to an official product site, handbook, changelog or source repository where practical.
2. A competitor screenshot alone is not enough evidence for an option contract.
3. Research must be refreshed when a surface reaches implementation or parity certification if the benchmark snapshot is stale.
4. WPE may intentionally reject unsafe competitor mechanics while preserving the use case through a safer provider/Ability boundary.
5. Product parity is capability parity, not visual cloning.
